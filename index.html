BEHIND THE SIGNAL

How the comparison works.

A straightforward method, with the evidence available to review.

01 / REPRESENT

More than exact words

Word unigrams and bigrams capture phrasing. Character 3–5-grams capture smaller textual patterns.

02 / COMPARE

Measure the overlap

TF-IDF weights distinctive terms. Cosine similarity blends word (65%) and character (35%) signals.

03 / REVIEW

Keep the context

Review sentence matches alongside their source evidence. Quotations and common phrases can also match.
